SPIDEROOMS LORE
The Spiderooms are not just endless rooms.
They are connected universes hidden behind unknown doors.
Some sectors are peaceful.
Others should never be entered.
📼 Season 1
Player enters the Spiderooms for the first time and discovers strange endless sectors hidden behind mysterious doors. While exploring the unknown hallways, a mysterious entity known as Spider begins watching from the darkness.
🌌 Season 2
The Spiderooms multiverse starts expanding. New universes, transmissions and anomalies begin connecting together. Hidden factions appear across the sectors while the network becomes more unstable.
🕷️ Season 3
POV starts suffering strange illusions caused by mysterious powers hidden inside the multiverse. Reality becomes distorted and it becomes difficult to know what is real and what is only an illusion.
